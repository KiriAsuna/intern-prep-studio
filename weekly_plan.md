# 10-Week Plan

The full course has about 57 hours of "nominal" time (about 15 of them are the drill lessons at the end of each chapter). A first pass realistically takes 1.5–2 times that, plus review and mock interviews, so the plan assumes 2–3 hours a day for 10 weeks.

| Week | Content | Lessons |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Python basics + drills | 00, 01a–01l |
| 2 | NumPy and pandas + drills | 02a–02k |
| 3 | SQL + drills | 03a–03j |
| 4 | Algorithms (part 1) | 04a–04e |
| 5 | Algorithms (part 2) + mock OA + drills | 04f–04m |
| 6 | Probability, statistical inference | 05a–06c |
| 7 | Linear regression, logistic regression | 07a–08c |
| 8 | A/B testing, machine learning | 09a–10c |
| 9 | Model evaluation, ML from scratch, case studies, research communication | 11a–13 (including 11c, 12b) |
| 10 | Final review, mock interviews, capstone | 14a–14c, 15, the three mocks |

## Daily template (2–3 hours)

- **1–2 new lessons** (60–100 minutes): type out every exercise yourself; don't just read the answers;
- **Redo old exercises** (20–30 minutes): from week 3 on, pick 2–3 earlier "write it yourself" exercises each day, click "Reset" and redo them without looking at the answer. Coding and SQL fade unless you repeat them;
- **Speak out loud** (10–15 minutes): from week 6 on, draw 5 questions a day on the "Interview Questions" page and answer them out loud.

## If time is short (an OA within two or three weeks)

Do these in order and put the rest on hold:

1. 01a–01h (Python basics)
2. 02b–02f (pandas)
3. 03a–03f (SQL)
4. 04a–04e (hash maps, two pointers, sliding window, stacks, binary search)
5. 14b (full mock OA)

The statistics and ML concept lessons (05a, 06a, 07a, 08a, 09a, 10a, 11a) can be read in spare moments.

The "Drills" lessons at the end of each chapter (01i–01l, 02g–02k, 03g–03j, 04k–04m, 05c–11c, 12b) are read-only: good for spare moments and a final pass before a test.

## Rule for mistakes

For every exercise you got wrong or needed the answer for, write three lines in that lesson's notes:

1. What I originally thought;
2. Where it went wrong;
3. What signal should make me think of the right approach next time.

## Where your records are stored

In the `user_data/` folder: `progress.json` (completion and pass records), `answers.json` (what you wrote in the code boxes), and `notes.json` (notes). The first launch each day saves a backup to `user_data/backups/`.
